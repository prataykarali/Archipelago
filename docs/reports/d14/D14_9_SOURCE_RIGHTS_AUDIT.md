# D14.9 source-specific rights audit — 2026-11-28

The companion [68-row register](D14_9_SOURCE_RIGHTS_AUDIT.csv) covers each document in the pinned `corpus_v3_d14` manifest. Every row records its title, source URL from the D14 inventory, pinned artifact SHA-256 as the local source version, registered license, source-specific or general terms evidence URL, three distinct use permissions, unresolved restrictions, and a recommendation. **An upstream version was not verified for the 23 included legacy arXiv/Hugging Face mirror papers; their source URL points to the mirror, not an individual original paper.** The audit does not turn the prior generic `APPROVED_OA` flag into permission.

## Findings

* **63 of 68 documents are quarantined, one is excluded, and four are conditional.** The 23 included papers labelled arXiv use a [license that grants arXiv distribution rights](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html); it does not itself grant this project passage redistribution rights. arXiv also states that [different versions can carry different licenses](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html). The 17 OSTEP chapters are free online, but the [author page asks users to link to chapters rather than copy them](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/). Other free-to-read, IETF, GFDL, and mixed-source records need document-specific conditions resolved.
* Source notices exposed four license-register conflicts: [*The Little Book of Semaphores*](https://greenteapress.com/semaphores/LittleBookOfSemaphores.pdf) states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, while the register says GFDL-1.2; [*Think OS*](https://greenteapress.com/thinkos/thinkos.pdf) and [*Think Stats*](https://greenteapress.com/thinkstats2/thinkstats2.pdf) state CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, while the register says CC BY-NC 3.0; the captured *Dive Into Systems* author page states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, while the register says CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. These records stay quarantined.
* The pinned [*Understanding Machine Learning* publisher PDF](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~shais/UnderstandingMachineLearning/understanding-machine-learning-theory-algorithms.pdf) explicitly restricts the copy to personal use and forbids distribution. It is **excluded from a hosted full-text corpus** absent written permission. Its register label “Author Free Open PDF” was not a redistribution grant.
* Four documents are **conditional** review candidates: [Jeff Erickson's *Algorithms*](https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/algorithms/) under CC BY 4.0 with backlink/attribution requirements; [SQLite documentation](https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html) under its public-domain dedication; [PostgreSQL documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/about/licence/) under the PostgreSQL License with notice requirements; and [LLVM documentation](https://llvm.org/LICENSE.txt) under Apache-2.0 with LLVM exception and notice requirements. Inclusion still requires confirming the app's displayed attribution and any third-party content in the extracted passages.
* The [IETF terms](https://www.ietf.org/process/ipr/) allow unmodified portions subject to restrictions; the five RFC records remain quarantined until the exact extracted passages and notices are checked. Creative Commons license terms alone do not prove that a particular source version carried the stated license, and noncommercial/share-alike conditions need deployment review.

Further pinned notices identify CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for Critchlow/Eck and Rosulek, CC BY-SA 4.0 for Levin, GFDL 1.3 or later for GNU Bison, and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 for *Pro Git*. Their terms permit some reuse, but attribution, share-alike, noncommercial, or full-license obligations have not been verified in the application. They remain quarantined.

No unfiltered V3 upload is authorized by this audit. See [its versioned manifest](D14_9_RIGHTS_FILTERED_CORPUS_MANIFEST.json). It is unsuitable for the 12-domain author retest and must not be deployed as a replacement.
