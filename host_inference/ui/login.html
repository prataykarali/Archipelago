Welcome to Archipelago

Privacy, Data Retention & Terms

Archipelago processes your institutional account identifier, role, chat queries, and saved research runs to deliver library & scholarly discovery services. Authentication is managed via Supabase Auth; scholarly metadata queries are sent to OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, and our AI inference gateway. No third-party advertising trackers are used. Sessions expire after inactivity or when the sign-in token reaches its lifetime. For account deletion or privacy inquiries, contact the IEM/UEM Library Administrator or clear your saved browser data below.
