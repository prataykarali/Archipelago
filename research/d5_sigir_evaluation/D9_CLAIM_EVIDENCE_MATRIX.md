## 1. Classification Taxonomy

Every scientific, empirical, architectural, and operational claim in the SIGIR 2027 manuscript ([`docs/sigir/manuscript/sigir2027_demo.tex`](docs/sigir/manuscript/sigir2027_demo.tex)) and evaluation reports is classified into one of four strict categories:

- **`VERIFIED`**: Deterministic, bit-for-bit reproducible from frozen SHA-256-locked artifacts, automated unit/integration tests, or authenticated D8 live production byte-level verification.
- **`PROVISIONAL`**: Reproducible from deterministic code or legacy audit with unconfirmed attribution records, but contingent on algorithmic candidate qrels (`human_reviewed=false`) or unconfirmed legacy self-review attribution (`independent_peer_review=false`). Must be explicitly qualified in the manuscript.
- **`UNVERIFIED`**: Protocol and tooling are complete and ready, but empirical values do not yet exist because independent human annotator worksheets (`731` items) have not been completed.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`**: Contradicted by runtime evaluation or deprecated from legacy v0/v1 reports; strictly prohibited from appearing as a positive claim in the manuscript.
