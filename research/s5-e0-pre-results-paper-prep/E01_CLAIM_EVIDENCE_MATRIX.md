**Audit Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Classification Taxonomy:**
- **`VERIFIED`**: Directly confirmed against frozen local artifacts with exact configuration, output, and commit/SHA-256 hash. Safe to report in pre-results manuscript sections within stated scope.
- **`PRELIMINARY`**: Observed in frozen pre-adjudication audit outputs, but subject to unresolved human-verification or independence gates. Reportable **only** as in-progress audit status with explicit caveats, never as final validated evidence.
- **`BLOCKED`**: Requires finalized human-adjudicated gold labels (`S5-C3` + `S5-D1`). Must **not** be computed, estimated, or asserted prior to gate clearance.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`**: Contradicted by the pilot design, sample size, or corpus scope. Permanently impermissible for the v2.1 pilot (even post-S5-D1).
