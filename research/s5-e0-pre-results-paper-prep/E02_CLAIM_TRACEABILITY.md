## 2. Critical Self-Review of E0.2 Framing

Before handing E0.2 to independent verification, we audited all three E0.2 drafting files against five common failure modes in pre-results IR manuscripts:

1. **Title-Scope Drift ("Academic Knowledge Discovery" vs. Technical CS Passage Retrieval):**
   - *Risk identified:* The provisional title's phrase *"for Academic Knowledge Discovery"* overstated both the task (ad-hoc passage ranking, not end-to-end knowledge discovery or synthesis) and the domain breadth (3 CS subfields over 53 documents, not multidisciplinary academic corpora).
   - *Resolution applied:* Evaluated explicitly in `E02_RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md` §1 and narrowed in `E02_INTRODUCTION_DRAFT.md` to *"An Auditable Pilot Evaluation of Lexical, Dense, Hybrid, and Graph-Based Passage Retrieval on a Technical Computer Science Corpus"*.
2. **Conflation of Graph Traversal Expansion with Retrieval Improvement:**
   - *Risk identified:* Describing 2-hop traversal reaching 167 extra paths and replacing up to 5 passages on `Q05` could be misread as demonstrating richer or better retrieval.
   - *Resolution applied:* `RQ3`, `E02_INTRODUCTION_DRAFT.md` (Contrib 2), and `E02_CONTRIBUTIONS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` (`C3`) explicitly label hop-2 expansion as **purely behavioral rank/set perturbation** and state that added passages may be either relevant context or topical distractors pending S5-D1 gold labels (`CLM-16`).
3. **Treating Pre-Adjudication Weighted Kappa ($\kappa_w = 0.7677$) as Validated Human IAA:**
   - *Risk identified:* Mentioning $\kappa_w = 0.7677$ in the Introduction without immediate qualification could mislead a reader into thinking inter-human agreement is already validated.
   - *Resolution applied:* Every mention of S5-C2 agreement in `E02_INTRODUCTION_DRAFT.md` and `E02_CONTRIBUTIONS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` immediately pairs it with Reviewer B's 254/254 AI-assistance disclosure, the 51 unresolved disagreements, and the formal S5-C2 `HOLD` status (`CLM-13`, `CLM-21`, `faggioli2023perspectives`).
4. **Latency Scope Consistency (`LIM-08`):**
   - *Risk identified:* Citing S5-B1.1 warm latencies (`12 queries × 5 reps`) alongside S5-B2.1 10-query diagnostics without distinguishing their scopes would contradict `S5D0_TECHNICAL_DIAGNOSTICS.md` §6.
   - *Resolution applied:* `E02_INTRODUCTION_DRAFT.md` (Limitations) and `E02_CONTRIBUTIONS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` (`C2`, §4) explicitly qualify warm CPU latency profiling as pre-revision S5-B1.1 12-query scope on 713 passages and disclaim large-corpus/GPU extrapolation (`CLM-11`, `CLM-22`).
5. **Citation & Numerical Integrity:**
   - *Check performed:* Every external citation key in all E0.2 files (`robertson1994some`, `robertson2009probabilistic`, `trotman2014improvements`, `reimers2019sentence`, `karpukhin2020dense`, `thakur2021beir`, `merrick2024arctic`, `cormack2009reciprocal`, `luan2021sparse`, `bruch2023analysis`, `xiong2017explicit`, `dietz2018utilizing`, `yasunaga2021qagnn`, `edge2024local`, `gutierrez2024hipporag`, `yang2018hotpotqa`, `min2019compositional`, `ho2020constructing`, `trivedi2022musique`, `cohen1968weighted`, `zobel1998reliable`, `voorhees2000variations`, `jarvelin2002cumulated`, `buckley2004retrieval`, `sakai2016statistical`, `faggioli2023perspectives`) is verified in `E03_VERIFIED_REFERENCES.bib`; zero unverified citations or unverified numbers were introduced, and all post-adjudication results are explicitly marked `TODO`.
